Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MUME Mobile (the "Application") handles data when used as a Discord Activity.

1. Data Collection

The Application runs entirely in the client-side browser environment. We do not operate databases or backend storage servers to collect, store, or log your personal data.

2. Discord Data

When you authorize the Application inside Discord, we utilize the Discord Embedded App SDK to access your basic public profile details (username, user ID, and avatar) solely to authenticate your local session and display your username/avatar in the game interface. This data is processed in-memory in your browser and is never stored, sold, or shared with third parties.

3. MUME MUD Connection

All gameplay data (vitals, commands, logs) flows directly between your client browser and the official MUME MUD servers. We do not intercept, monitor, or store any of your game credentials or character details.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use standard browser LocalStorage to save your client preferences (such as UI themes, custom button sets, and sound settings) locally on your device for your convenience.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
