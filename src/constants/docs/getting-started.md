# Getting Started

Get ready to adventure in Middle-earth! Here is the minimal guide to playing MUME with this client.

## 🧭 Basic Movement
- **Tactile keys:** On desktop, use Arrow keys or WASD to move.
- **Smart Walk:** Click/tap or hold a room on the map to pathfind there automatically. Release to stop.
- **Vertical Exits:** Exits going up or down are shown with small arrow indicators on the map.

## 🎯 Targeting & Combat
- **Set Target:** Tap any player, NPC, or monster name in the text log or room list to set them as your target.
- **Manual Target:** Tap the target status bar at the top to type a target manually.
- **Vitals:** HP, Mana, and Moves bars are docked at the bottom. Tap them for status details.

## 💬 Chatting
- **Parley Bar:** Focused channels (say, tell, group) appear above the command input when typing.
- **Spat Reply:** Quick tap buttons appear in the log next to incoming messages to reply instantly.
