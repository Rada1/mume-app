# Getting Started with MUME

Welcome to the MUME MUD Client! This guide will help you understand the core features available for both desktop and mobile adventurers.

## 🧭 Movement & Mapping
- **Smart Walk**: Long-press a room on the map to automatically travel there. Release your hold at any time to stop.
- **Dynamic Exploration**: Newly explored areas fade in smoothly. Adjacent unexplored rooms are partially visible through the "peek" effect.
- **Visual Indicators**: The mapper marks connections, quest locations (?), and vertical exits (▲/▼).

## 🎯 Targeting & Vitals
- **Target Lock**: Tap a character's name in the message log to set them as your current target.
- **Modern Vitals**: Real-time HP, Mana, and Moves bars are docked at the bottom. Tap them for a quick `score` or `stat` check.

## 💬 Communication
- **Parley Bar**: When communicating, a specialized bar appears above your command bar to quickly toggle between `say`, `tell`, and other channels, with easy target selection.
- **Spat Buttons**: Contextual communication buttons appear when you receive messages, allowing for one-tap replies.

## 🎮 Mobile Gestures
- **Side Drawers**: Swipe **west (left)** on the command bar to open your drawers: Inventory/Equipment, Stats, Character, and Players.
- **Peeking**: A subtle swipe up on the command bar "peeks" the map, while a swipe down peeks your character info.
- **Quick Command**: Swipe **southwest** on the command bar to quickly send a common utility command.

## 🛠️ Customization
- **Design Mode**: Enter Design Mode from the main menu to move UI clusters and edit action buttons. 
- **Button Editor**: In Design Mode, tap any button to change its command, label, color, or icon.
- **Persistance**: All layouts and buttons are saved per-character.

## 🔌 Connection
- **Auto-Reconnect**: The client handles intermittent connections. If disconnected, a red **Reconnect** button will appear in the top-right header.
